06 — Straight answers

Questions, answered honestly.

Do I have to change the tools I already use?
No. That's the whole point. The dashboard sits on top of what you already run and pulls it together. If a tool is genuinely holding you back we'll say so, but we're not going to make you migrate for the sake of it.
What if I already have a CRM?
Good — we'll connect to it. The dashboard isn't a replacement for your CRM, it's the layer that makes your CRM, your job data and your numbers agree with each other.
How long until it's actually live?
Usually three to six weeks from the scope being signed off, depending on how many systems are involved. You'll be using parts of it well before the whole thing is finished.
What happens after it's built?
We host it, support it and keep it running. Integrations break when other companies change their APIs — that's covered. Small changes are included; anything big gets quoted first.
What if I'm not technical?
Then you're exactly who this is for. You won't be configuring anything or reading documentation. You get something you open and use, and we handle everything behind it.
