03 — Straight answers

Questions,
answered honestly.

What would you actually build for me?
Whatever's costing you the most money right now. If enquiries are thin, that's the marketing side — ads and campaigns that put you in front of people who actually buy. If enquiries come in but go cold, that's the sales and AI side — instant replies, follow-ups that actually happen, quotes that get chased. Most businesses have one of those bleeding badly. We fix that one first, then build out the rest once it's paying for itself.
Do you only work with trades?
No. We work with any business where the job starts with an enquiry and ends with a booking — trades, cleaning, health and beauty, coaches, consultants, agencies, professional services. Different industries, same problem: good leads going quiet because nobody got back to them fast enough.
Do I have to change the software I already use?
No. We build around what you've got — your phone number, your inbox, your calendar, your job management software. If something you're using is genuinely holding you back we'll tell you, but you won't be relearning your own business on new tools. And you don't need to be technical: we build it, we set it up, and we hand you something you can actually use.
Won't the AI sound like a robot?
Not the way we set it up. It replies in your words, with your prices and your rules — not a generic chatbot script. It handles the repetitive part: answering, qualifying, booking, chasing the follow-up. Anything it's unsure about comes straight to you with the full conversation attached. You can read every message it sends, and change how it responds whenever you like.
What do you need from me?
An hour or two at the start, so we understand how you quote, price and follow up. After that we build, and you review everything before it goes near a customer. The whole point is that this takes work off your plate — if you're spending more time on it than you were before, we've built the wrong thing.
How fast does this happen?
The first call and the bottleneck audit can happen this week. Building usually starts within a week or two after that, and we get the first piece live before we move on to the next one. You'll have something working and earning early, instead of waiting months for everything to land at once.
What does it cost?
It depends what we build. Clearing one bottleneck costs a lot less than a full marketing, sales and AI setup, and we're not going to throw a number at you before we know which one you need. What we will do is give you a fixed price in writing after the first call, so you know exactly what you're spending before anything starts. No retainer creep, no surprise invoices.
What happens on the first call?
About half an hour, no pitch deck. You tell us where things feel stuck, we work out what's actually causing it, and we tell you what we'd fix first. If we're not the right people for the job, we'll say so on the call rather than sell you something that won't work. It's free either way, and you'll leave knowing what's holding you back whether you hire us or not.
