Straight answers

Questions,
answered honestly.

What do you actually do?
Two things. Client Acquisition brings the work in and closes it: your offer priced so it is worth replying to, ads built from your real work, and the sales process behind them. AI keeps the back end and fulfilment tight: follow ups that fire whether or not you remember, quotes chased until they are answered, job admin off your evenings, and a daily read on your numbers. Take one or take both. Most people take both, because more enquiries coming in is exactly what makes work start slipping out the back.
What are the two ways of working?
Done alongside you is a single day where I teach you to build it. Your hands on the keyboard, mine off it, and I stay on it with you for the thirty days after. Done for you is where I build the same machine and then run it, month to month, and you do not touch it. Same build either way. The only difference is whose hands are on it and who runs it afterwards.
Which one should I pick?
If you want control and a fixed one off cost, done alongside you. If you would rather buy the outcome than learn the tools, done for you. If you are torn, tell me which hurts more: not enough work coming in, or work slipping while you are out on the tools. That usually answers it in about a minute, and I will tell you on the call if I think you are picking the wrong one.
Do you only work with trades?
Mostly trades and home services, because that is what I run myself and what the systems were built on. The same setup works for any business where the job starts with an enquiry and ends with a booking, so I do take on cleaners, health and beauty, consultants and professional services when the fit is right. Different industries, same problem: good leads going quiet because nobody got back to them fast enough.
Do I have to change the software I already use?
No. It gets built around what you have got: your phone number, your inbox, your calendar, your job management software. If something you are using is genuinely holding you back I will tell you, but you will not be relearning your own business on new tools. You do not need to be technical either way.
Will the AI sound like a robot?
Not the way I set it up. It replies in your words, with your prices and your rules, not a generic chatbot script. It is worth being clear about what it does and does not do: it handles the repetitive part, answering fast, chasing quotes that go quiet, asking for reviews and keeping the admin moving. It does not close jobs. Sales does that, and that part stays yours. Anything it is unsure about comes straight to you with the full conversation attached.
What if the AI gets something wrong?
It can only quote the prices and rules you gave it. Anything it has not been told, it says you will come back to them and sends you the thread. Every message it sends is written out in plain English before it goes live, so you know exactly what it says to your customers and why, and you can change how it responds whenever you like.
How fast does this happen?
Done alongside you is one day, and if ads are part of it they are spending real money by lunch. Dates are limited because it is one client per day and I run my own business around them. Done for you starts with a twenty minute call and an audit, then a written scope and price within forty eight hours, and the build follows from there.
What does it cost?
Done alongside you is one payment, agreed in writing before anything starts, with thirty days of support included. Done for you is quoted after a call and an audit, because what it costs to run depends on your services, your margins and how much work you can take, and quoting that blind would be guessing with your money. Ad spend and your own AI subscription are separate, paid by you directly, and never pass through me. Ask me for whichever document fits and the full numbers are in it.
What if it doesn't work?
Nobody can promise you a number from ads or AI, and anyone who does is guessing with your money. What I can promise: you see the whole thing running on a real business, mine, before you pay a cent. The scope and price are in writing before anything starts. Everything is in your name, so nothing is rented from me and nothing breaks if you walk.
